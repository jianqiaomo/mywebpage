Research Interests

My research advances data privacy and integrity through hardware-software co-design. I build efficient systems that make cryptographic privacy and integrity practical.

  • Privacy-Preserving Computation for End-to-End Data Privacy: I develop specialized hardware systems to accelerate privacy-preserving computation. My work includes accelerators for cryptography (e.g., homomorphic encryption), particularly Garbled Circuits (GC) [1], [2], which enable accurate private ML inference.
    Applying privacy-preserving computation to private inference.
  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Data Integrity: I develop efficient proof systems and programmable hardware accelerators for zero-knowledge proofs (ZKPs). ZKPs verify data integrity by proving the correctness of computations without revealing the underlying data.
    An example of a zero-knowledge proof.
