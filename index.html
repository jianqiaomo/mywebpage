Research Interests

My research advances data privacy and integrity through hardware-software co-design. I build efficient systems that make cryptographic privacy and integrity practical.

  • Privacy-Preserving Computation for End-to-End Data Privacy: secure machine learning and private inference using techniques such as garbled circuits and cryptographic hardware acceleration.
  • Zero-Knowledge Proofs for Data Integrity: efficient proof systems and programmable hardware accelerators for verifiable computation.
